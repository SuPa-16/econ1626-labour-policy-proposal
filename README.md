## Overview

This repository contains a 1,200-word evidence-based policy proposal advising the **Deputy Secretary, Employment & Skills, Australian Government** on how to respond to AI's growing impacts on the Australian labour market.

The proposal argues that AI is reshaping — not simply eliminating — Australian jobs, creating a structural skills mismatch that requires targeted government intervention. It evaluates three policy options and recommends a combined **National AI Reskilling Fund** and **Portable Learning Entitlements** scheme, supported by a phased 36-month implementation plan.
